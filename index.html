About the Author

Nivetha Kannan

Author of Love That Was Never Spoken

I'm a graduate in English Literature and an aspiring writer from Tamil Nadu, India. I've always been drawn to the beauty of words, emotions, and stories that stay with us long after we finish reading them.

My passion for literature began as a love for reading and gradually grew into a dream of becoming a writer. I enjoy exploring human emotions, relationships, love, and the feelings we often struggle to put into words.

Debut Novel

Love That Was Never Spoken

A romance that explores the depth of unspoken emotions, silent love, and the feelings that remain hidden between people. The story reflects the idea that some of the strongest emotions are the ones we never find the courage — or the opportunity — to say aloud.

Love That Was Never Spoken is now published and available for readers. With my first book, I hope to connect with readers who have ever loved silently, waited quietly, or carried words in their hearts that were never spoken.

Beyond writing, I'm passionate about English literature, learning, creativity, and building a meaningful journey as a young author. This is only the beginning, and I hope to create many more stories that readers can see themselves in.
